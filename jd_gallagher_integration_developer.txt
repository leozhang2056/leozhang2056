About the role:

Reporting to the Integration Architect, this position is responsible for the design, development, testing, and support of software solutions within delivery teams at Gallagher Insurance New Zealand (AJG NZ). The role contributes to the delivery of high-quality applications, APIs, integrations, and automation solutions that align with agreed technical standards, architectural direction, and security practices.

The Developer works closely with other developers, testers, DevOps engineers, business analysts, and architects to deliver maintainable, secure, and reliable solutions across a range of technologies and platforms.

Key responsibilities for the role include:

Contribute to the design, development, testing, maintenance, and support of applications, APIs, integrations, and supporting services
Develop solutions across AJG technology stacks, including MuleSoft, Java/Spring Boot, Node.js, front-end applications, and integrations with SaaS platforms (e.g. Salesforce, Unmand)
Develop and maintain automated tests, including unit, integration, and regression test suites where appropriate
Participate in code reviews and contribute to maintaining high standards of code quality, performance, security, and maintainability
Contribute technical input during refinement, including solution options, estimates, technical dependencies, and implementation considerations
Support release activities, including deployments, smoke testing, validation, and post-release monitoring
Investigate and resolve defects, incidents, and production support issues in a timely manner
Work with DevOps engineers to ensure solutions are buildable, deployable, observable, and operationally ready
Follow agreed development standards, secure coding practices, and architectural guidelines
Produce and maintain technical documentation where required
Collaborate effectively with developers, testers, business analysts, product owners, and architects to deliver business outcomes
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives across development practices, tooling, automation, and the SDLC

Skills and Experience:

Hands-on experience with one or more of the AJG technology stacks, including MuleSoft, Java/Spring Boot, Node.js, React or similar front-end frameworks
Experience developing REST APIs, integrations, and service-based solutions
Experience working with SaaS platform integrations (e.g. Salesforce, Unmand) is desirable
Experience developing and maintaining automated tests and supporting quality assurance activities
Understanding of software development principles, secure coding practices, and source control processes
Understanding of emerging AI technologies and the ability to leverage AI-assisted tools and agents to support software development, testing, troubleshooting, and delivery activities
Experience supporting software releases and production issue resolution
Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, automated deployment processes, and DevOps practices
Experience working in Agile delivery environments
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Ability to work collaboratively within cross-functional delivery teams
3+ years' experience in software development, integration, or related technical roles
