What you will be doing | Nga whainga matua

This is a unique opportunity for an experienced Software Developer to support the Kiwifruit Data Programme and the Kiwifruit Breeding Centre. You will develop and maintain science informatics systems used to capture, manage, analyse, and deliver breeding and research data that supports the development of future kiwifruit cultivars.

Working closely with scientists, breeders, Technical Product Owners and other software developers, you will:

Design, develop, test and maintain software applications and services.
Support data capture and analysis platforms used within kiwifruit breeding and research programmes.
Develop REST APIs, database solutions, and modern web applications.
Participate in Agile software delivery practices.
Contribute to software quality through automated testing, peer reviews and continuous improvement.
Collaborate with scientists and breeding teams to deliver practical, user-focused solutions.
