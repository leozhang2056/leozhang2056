Experience designing and delivering complex enterprise applications

Experience with Agile delivery, automated testing, CI/CD, and modern SDLC practices

Azure or AWS
DevOps and CI/CD pipelines
Distributed systems and integrations
Salesforce or other CRM platforms
AI-assisted development tools
Insurance or financial services

Hybrid working

The chance to work on meaningful products used by thousands of New Zealanders
