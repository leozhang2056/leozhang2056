Principal Engineer

About The Opportunity

This is a high-impact Principal Engineer opportunity within a large, complex environment undergoing significant transformation. You'll join a squad with strong technical leadership and a clear roadmap, focused on modernising core platforms and advancing capability.

While there is exposure to nCino and Salesforce, this is not an nCino Engineer role. This is a true Principal position for someone who can operate across engineering, integration, data and architecture, taking ownership of standards, guiding best practice, and influencing how systems are built at scale.

You'll play a key role in shaping how the organisation evolves its technology landscape bringing a forward-thinking mindset across data, integration, and emerging technologies like AI, while navigating regulatory and enterprise complexity within a banking environment.

What You'll Be Doing

This role sits across both strategy and delivery, ideal for someone who enjoys influencing direction while staying close to the technology.

Lead across the development layer, uplifting engineering standards, principles, and best practices.
Drive integration across Salesforce and broader platforms, including API design and service architecture.
Act as a thought leader, guiding architectural decisions and ensuring alignment with regulatory and enterprise standards.
Shape and embed CI/CD pipelines, development frameworks, and engineering governance.
Work cross-functionally to deliver scalable, resilient solutions across complex systems.
Provide direction across data architecture, system design, and integration strategy.
Support the adoption of AI and modern technologies as part of the broader roadmap.
Influence senior stakeholders and bring clarity to complex technical and business challenges.

The Technology Stack

You'll be working across a modern, evolving ecosystem that blends core banking platforms with scalable integration and data capabilities:

Salesforce – A core platform underpinning customer and workflow capabilities, with a strong focus on integration and extensibility
nCino – Supporting lending and banking workflows; exposure is important, but this role is not hands-on nCino configuration
API & Integration frameworks – Designing and enabling robust integrations across internal systems and third-party platforms (REST APIs, event-driven architecture, microservices)
CI/CD & Engineering Practices – Mature delivery pipelines, automation, and a focus on uplifting engineering standards, release processes, and quality
Data Architecture & Distributed Systems – Working across complex data environments, ensuring systems are scalable, well-structured, and aligned to enterprise data strategy.

AI / automation – Interest or experience in leveraging AI, machine learning, or automation to enhance platforms and customer outcomes
