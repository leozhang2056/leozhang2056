About the role

The Junior Developer role will work in collaboration with the development team to create high-level programs that meet the client's needs. Reporting to the Senior Developer, this role works across a variety of projects, infrastructure and languages. The Junior Developer will work with talented developers on new and existing products that keep the creatives happy. As well as that, the role will also work on internal tools and documentation to enable our Operations team to be as effective as possible.

About us

The Rebel Fleet is dedicated to giving creatives the freedom to create by partnering with producers, studios and production teams to simplify the complex workflows behind film, television and commercial productions.
Konsol is The Rebel Fleet's technology platform, built from years of hands-on industry experience and a deep understanding of the challenges faced by modern production teams. It brings people, information and workflows together in one connected environment, helping productions operate more efficiently from set through to post-production.
The Rebel Fleet has a growing portfolio of exciting projects and ambitious plans. Joining the Konsol team means helping shape technology that is changing how screen productions collaborate, communicate and deliver.
