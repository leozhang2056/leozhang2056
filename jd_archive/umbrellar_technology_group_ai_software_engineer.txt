About the Role
We're looking for a Software Engineer who builds AI-powered products and automation on the Microsoft stack. This is a hands-on engineering role at the centre of our client delivery work, building agentic applications, automation platforms, and integrations across Azure and Microsoft 365.

You'll work in .NET and Python, with PostgreSQL as the data layer and Azure AI Foundry as the AI platform. You'll partner with our data engineering and architecture teams, and you'll own what you build from investigation through to production.

This is an AI-native engineering role. We expect you to direct AI coding agents as part of your daily workflow, build agentic systems for clients, and make pragmatic calls about where AI genuinely helps and where it doesn't.

This is a customer-facing, consulting role. You'll work directly with clients running discovery to understand their business, actively consulting to surface their real needs, and shaping the solutions worth building alongside them.
