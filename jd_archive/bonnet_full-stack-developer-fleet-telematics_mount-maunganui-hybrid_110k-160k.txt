This is an exciting opportunity for an experienced Full Stack Developer to join the technology team at BONNET, reporting directly to our CTO. This role is predominantly backend-focused (approximately 80%), with 20% frontend development, however a strong capability and pride in delivering high-quality frontend work is essential.

This role is open to candidates based anywhere in New Zealand. While you can work remotely, monthly trips to our Mount Maunganui office will be required. All travel expenses will be fully covered.

You will play a key role in building, scaling, and improving BONNET's core platforms and products.

About BONNET

BONNET is a fleet compliance management platform with built-in telematics. We help businesses manage vehicles, machines, and drivers by automating compliance and eliminating the manual admin that leads to fines, downtime, and operational risk.

BONNET combines:

Fleet compliance management, including WOF, COF, Rego, RUC, servicing, inspections, and documentation

Built-in telematics and GPS tracking, capturing real-time vehicle and machine data

Automated alerts, workflows, and reporting, giving fleet managers clear visibility of what's due, what's overdue, and what needs action

Our platform is used by construction companies, trades, logistics operators, rental fleets, and enterprise customers managing anything from a handful of vehicles through to large, complex fleets. BONNET is built to scale and sits at the intersection of software, vehicle data, and real-world operations.

What you'll be doing

Collaborate closely with the CTO and wider technology team to define technical requirements, system architecture, and product roadmaps

Our stack

The BONNET backend is a LAMP stack in the cloud. We use PHP with CodeIgniter and MySQL to power our web applications, along with a data API that supports our Android (Kotlin) and iOS (Swift) apps.
