AKQA - Senior Native App Developer

At AKQA (part of WPP) our Technology discipline sets the approach and standard for native app delivery and governance, helping deliver a world-class application through a mature agile methodology.

As a Senior Native App Developer, you will bring deep native iOS and/or Android experience into a collaborative, multidisciplinary team. You'll help build and evolve a world-class mobile application, balancing craft, quality and pace, while supporting the team with clear thinking and practical guidance.

Role Requirements:
- Deliver native mobile development end-to-end across the full app life cycle
- Define the work needed to implement solutions; assess and communicate feasibility and delivery impact
- Estimate accurately, report progress clearly, and deliver to deadlines
- Produce high-quality code that is performant, reliable, and maintainable
- Partner with Experience Design to deliver high-fidelity, platform-appropriate UI that meets requirements
- Integrate with external systems and APIs; optimise app and tool performance
- Debug confidently, explain app flow to others, and triage/fix defects
- Contribute to continuous improvement, grow new skills on the job, and share knowledge openly within the team

Qualities & Characteristics:
- 5+ years building native apps with Swift and/or Kotlin
- Strong grasp of common frameworks, libraries, and design patterns; solid iOS and/or Android SDK knowledge
- Experience with RESTful APIs, multi-threading, and writing unit-tested, testable code
- Comfortable with DevOps/continuous delivery and agile ways of working
- Proven track record delivering high-quality iOS and/or Android applications

Hybrid in-studio/WFH model. On-site. Full-time. Auckland, NZ.
