Tō tūranga hou | Your new role

You will be responsible for delivering mobile banking solutions to clients across the full project lifecycle. This includes pre-sales support, requirements and gap analysis, project coordination, branding, product enhancement and extension, localisation, system integration, testing and deployment.

As a Software Developer, you will contribute across all stages of delivery, with a strong focus on developing enhancements and extensions to existing products.

The role may require occasional out-of-hours support in response to critical incidents or essential business needs.

Support the development of high-quality mobile software as part of an agile scrum team.
Contribute to strong code quality by maintaining and improving software development standards.
Support technical design decisions by validating approaches and prototyping new concepts.
Work closely with team members to help achieve successful sprint outcomes.
Encourage and mentor junior team members to support their ongoing development.
Proactively identify and raise technical, security, delivery and performance risks.
Actively contribute to and help promote a DevOps culture.
Identify and implement continuous improvements to development practices.
