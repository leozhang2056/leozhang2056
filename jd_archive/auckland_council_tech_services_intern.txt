Auckland Council's Summer Internship Programme offers paid, hands-on experience contributing to services and initiatives that help shape the future of Tāmaki Makaurau. For students in second-to-last year of study or continuing into a master's programme.

The Tech Services / ICT interns are involved in a mix of on-the-job learning and stretch projects, with work allocation depending on business need and the incumbent's learning and development.

What you'll be doing:
- Provide technical support and troubleshoot issues
- Assist with hardware and software maintenance
- System monitoring and documentation
- Contribute to relevant activities and projects as required
- Spend time in an agreed Technology Services team
- Exposure to Service management team through Service Desk

Requirements:
- In second-to-last year of relevant bachelor's degree, or continuing into master's degree
- Eligible to work in NZ full-time (23 Nov 2026 - 19 Feb 2027)
- Studying Information Technology / Information Systems / Software Engineering / Computer Sciences
- Enthusiastic about learning and contributing to different ICT Teams
- Keen interest in Technology/IT Support/Systems
- Information Literacy and interest in problem solving & data analysis
- Ability to contribute valuable ideas, actively listen, seek clarity, meet deadlines

Benefits:
- Hands-on experience, build practical skills
- Real insight into Auckland Council mahi
- Support from team, connection with other interns
- High-performing interns may get priority consideration for two-year Graduate Programme
